# Grandma's Banana Bread

Ingredients: three ripe bananas, one third cup of melted butter, one egg,
three quarters cup of sugar, one teaspoon of baking soda, one and a half cups of flour.

Mash the bananas and mix in the melted butter.
Stir in the sugar, the egg and the baking soda, then fold in the flour.
Bake in a loaf pan at 350 degrees for about one hour.
